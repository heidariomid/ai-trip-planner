Privacy policy

AI Trip Planner · Last updated 9 October 2026

In short

AI Trip Planner keeps the trips you plan so you can come back to them. We store what you type and upload, we send parts of it to a few services that make the app work, and we do not sell it or show you ads. You can delete your account, and everything with it, from the Profile tab at any time.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, your name, and a password that is stored only as a hash we cannot read. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google instead of a password.
  • Your profile: a picture if you upload one, and the interests you pick.
  • Your trips: titles, places, dates, descriptions, itineraries, bookings you write down (flights, car rentals, hotels, and their confirmation codes), packing lists, notes, cover pictures, and photos you add.
  • Your conversations with the assistant: the messages you send and the replies, so you can reopen them.
  • Places you save with the heart, by name and city.
  • Sharing: which trips you joined or shared, and with what role.
  • Usage counts: how many assistant messages and AI covers you used today, to keep the daily limits.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or payment details, and the app has no advertising or analytics trackers.

The assistant currently runs on a free tier of Google Gemini, under which Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products. Do not type anything into the chat that you would not want shared that way, such as passport numbers or card details.

The assistant and the AI covers run on free allowances that everyone using the app shares. When one runs out for the day, that feature stops answering until the next day.

Who can see your trips

Only you, until you share a trip. When you give someone a trip's join code, they can see that trip, including its bookings, notes, and photos, along with your name and profile picture. They never see your email address or your other trips. You can remove a person or change the code at any time.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. "Delete account" on the Profile tab removes your account, your trips, your files, and your conversations straight away and cannot be undone. A trip you shared is removed for the people you shared it with too. Photos you added to someone else's trip stay with that trip.

On your device

The app keeps you signed in by storing a session in your browser or on your phone. It uses no advertising cookies.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
