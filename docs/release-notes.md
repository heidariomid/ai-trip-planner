# Release notes

## 1.0.0 — first public release (October 2026)

The app is live at **https://plan-trip-ai.vercel.app**.

It is a web app. Open the address on a phone, add it to the home screen,
and it opens full screen like an app. There is no App Store or Google
Play build.

### What you can do

- Sign up with email and password, or continue with Google
- Create a trip with a cover, and build its itinerary day by day
- Record flights, car rentals, hotels, and extras
- Keep a packing list, a photo gallery, and notes for each trip
- Copy a curated plan from Explore into a trip of your own
- Chat with an AI assistant that proposes trips, days, places, bookings,
  and packing lists, and save any of them with one tap
- Generate a cover picture for a trip with AI
- Save places the assistant recommends
- Share a trip with a join code, as a viewer or an editor
- Edit your profile, read the privacy policy, and delete your account

### Known limits

- **Email addresses are not confirmed.** An account can be created with
  an address nobody checked.
- **A forgotten password cannot be recovered yet.** There is no password
  reset. Sign in with Google, or keep the password somewhere safe.
- **The AI runs on free allowances that every traveller shares.** When
  the assistant's or the cover generator's allowance is used up for the
  day, that feature answers with a short message and works again the
  next day. On top of that, each account is limited to 30 assistant
  messages and 5 generated covers a day.
- **The assistant uses a free tier of Google Gemini**, under which Google
  may use what is sent to it to improve its products. Do not type
  passport numbers, card details, or anything else sensitive into the
  chat.
- **Place photos come from Wikipedia**, so small restaurants and hotels
  often show a placeholder or a photo of the city instead.
- **It needs a connection.** Nothing works offline.
- **No notifications.**
- **Changing the address would sign everyone out**, so this address is
  meant to stay.
