## Title

I shipped a trip planner that only saves what you tap

## Body

The product: you say where you are going, and you get a day-by-day itinerary, places, bookings, and a packing list. The assistant proposes. Saving is a button, because I did not want a model writing into someone's trip on its own.

It is a website you can put on a home screen. No app store. No price. Signing up is the only way in. There is no demo account.

Stack is Expo and Supabase. Row-level security is what keeps one person's trips private, including trips they have shared with family as a viewer or an editor. AI is on free shared allowances, so launch day can run them out. The screen says the limit was reached instead of pretending the app broke.

What I would like feedback on: the first screen. Does it tell you what the product does before you scroll?
