# Peerlist

## Title

AI Trip Planner — one sentence in, a day-by-day trip out

## Body

I shipped a web app that plans a trip and then gets out of the way.

You describe the trip. The assistant streams a reply and attaches cards: the trip, each day, places with photos, bookings, and a packing list. A tap writes the card into your account. The model never writes on its own.

You can edit a stop, share the trip with a join code, and generate a cover. Add it to a phone's home screen and it opens full screen.

Built with Expo, Supabase, and Tailwind. Chat is Gemini. Covers are FLUX on Cloudflare's free allowance.
