## Name

AI Trip Planner

## Tagline

A day-by-day trip from one sentence you can edit

## Description

Tell it where you are going. It answers with the days, places, bookings, and a packing list. Nothing is saved until you tap. Free, in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen.

## URL

https://plan-trip-ai.vercel.app

## First comment

I built AI Trip Planner because a chat reply is a bad place to keep a trip.

You describe the trip. The assistant streams a plan and attaches cards for the days, the places, a hotel, and what to pack. A tap saves the card into an account that is yours. You can edit a day, tick things off, share the trip with family, and generate a cover.

Who it is for: someone planning their own trip, who may later invite another person as a viewer or an editor.

It is free. There is no ads and no card required. Two limits, so they are not a surprise: the assistant and the cover pictures share a free daily allowance, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered yet.

I would like to know whether the first screen explains the product, and whether the plan it gives you is one you would actually edit.
